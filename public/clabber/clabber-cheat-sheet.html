Melds — bonus combos in your hand

Four Jacks "mule"200
Four of a kind 9s, As, 10s, Ks, or Qs100
Run of five one suit, in order100
Run of four "fifty"50
Run of three "dad"20

Sequence order for runs is A K Q J 10 9. A card can only be used in one meld.

Bell = King + Queen of trumps, worth 20. It is special: it always scores, even if the other team out-melds you.
Dad 'n' Bell = a trump run of three that contains the K-Q (A-K-Q or K-Q-J). The dad and the bell stack for 40.

Announce meld just before you play to the first trick. Only the team with the single highest meld scores their melds; the other team scores none (Bell excepted).

3

How a hand plays out

Deal

Six cards to each player. The dealer's last card is turned face up to propose trump.

Bid

Starting left of dealer, each player says "I play" to take the up-card's suit as trump, or passes. First "I play" ends bidding.

Meld

On the first trick, announce your scoring combos before playing a card.

Play six tricks

Follow suit, win tricks with valuable cards. Winner of last trick earns +10.

Score

Add card points, melds, and Bell. First team to 500 wins.

4

Bidding

  • You must hold at least one card of the suit you name as trump.
  • If everyone passes, the dealer takes the up-card and a second round opens: now you may name any other suit as trump.
  • All pass twice → no score, redeal to the next dealer.
  • Whoever says "I play" makes the contract: their team must out-score the other.
5

Following suit & trumping

  • Always follow the suit led if you can.
  • Can't follow? You must play a trump if you hold one.
  • Once trumps are in the trick, you must play a higher trump if able — even when your partner is winning.
  • Following a plain-suit lead, you do not have to beat it.
  • Highest trump wins; with no trump, highest card of the led suit wins.
6

Scoring & getting "set"

  • Each team totals their card points + melds + Bell, and the last-trick team adds 10.
  • If the trump-making team scores more than their opponents, both teams keep what they earned.
  • If the makers tie or fall short, they score zero and the opponents take everything. The makers are "set" or "hicked."
  • With no melds in play, the makers need 82+ of the 162 points to stay safe.

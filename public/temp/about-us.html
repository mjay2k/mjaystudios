§3 THE ARGUMENT · WARM WHITE · REUSE SPLIT BLOCK
Why it matters

The handoff is where things break.

The motor builder blames the drive. The drive vendor blames the controls. The controls integrator blames the motor. Meanwhile your line is down and nobody owns it.

FLANDERS designs, manufactures, installs, and services all three. When something goes wrong there's exactly one number to call — and the people who answer it built the thing.

That's not a philosophy, it's an org chart. The same engineers who spec a custom motor sign off on its rebuild five years later. The same field crew that commissions a drive takes the 2 a.m. call. Fewer handoffs, fewer seams, and nowhere for accountability to fall through. (If you want the specifics — horsepower ranges, certifications, what we'll work on — most of it's in the FAQ.)

§6 FOOTPRINT · DOORWAY → /LOCATIONS
Where we are

We don't fly in from headquarters.

Evansville, Indiana has been home base since 1947 — central management, advanced manufacturing, specialty machine shops, CNC mills, and dynamometers capable of testing the largest motors we build.

But the machines we service don't come to Indiana. So we put Regional Service Centers where the work is: Tucson, Lakeland, Longview, and Casper. Brisbane and Bayswater in Australia. Middelburg in South Africa. Field offices in Itabira, Antofagasta, Lima, and Arequipa.

Each one is staffed with power and control specialists and equipped for emergency support, diagnostics, repairs, modernization, and commissioning — locally, not eventually.

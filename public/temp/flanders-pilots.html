Mining

Powering Productivity For Mining Machines

From Motors to Automation — FLANDERS supports surface and underground mining operations with rugged electrical systems, advanced automation, and full-lifecycle services designed to keep critical equipment moving. From drills, shovels, and draglines to haul trucks and auxiliary systems, our engineered and purpose-built motors, drives, control platforms, and fleet technologies are engineered to withstand extreme loads, harsh conditions, and continuous duty cycles — improving uptime, safety, and operational efficiency across the mine site.

Equipment We Support

  • Electric motors and drive systems for drills, shovels, draglines, and crushers
  • Haul truck electrification and hybrid drive systems for surface mining fleets
  • Fleet monitoring and control platforms for performance, diagnostics, and safety
  • Auxiliary electrical systems supporting pumps, conveyors, fans, and ventilation
  • Power distribution equipment including switchgear, transformers, and MCCs

Challenges We Solve

  • Unplanned downtime from motor, drive, or electrical system failures
  • Equipment stress caused by extreme loads, shock, dust, heat, and vibration
  • Aging electrical infrastructure reducing reliability and production capacity
  • Energy inefficiency increasing operating and fuel costs
  • Safety risks tied to manual processes or outdated control systems
  • Difficulty maintaining uptime during continuous, 24/7 operations
  • OEM obsolescence impacting long-term serviceability and parts availability

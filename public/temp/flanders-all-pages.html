Electrical Testing & Diagnostics

source: https://flanders.mjaystudios.com/electrical-testing-diagnostics

ELECTRICAL TESTING & DIAGNOSTICS

Identify Electrical Risk Before Failure Occurs

Electrical and rotating equipment rarely fail without warning — heat, imbalance, insulation breakdown, and loose connections develop over time. FLANDERS field testing and diagnostics detect faults early, quantify risk, and support informed maintenance decisions before a failure forces the issue.

Early detection lowers repair costs and reduces production disruption.

Common fault indicators include:​​

  • Motor winding degradation
  • Insulation breakdown
  • Bearing wear and imbalance
  • Loose or overloaded connections
  • Phase imbalance and power quality issues
  • Harmonic distortion affecting drives and controls

Diagnostic Services

  • ​​Vibration Analysis: Detects imbalance, misalignment, bearing defects, and mechanical looseness in motors and rotating equipment.
  • Motor Circuit Analysis (PdMA): Evaluates rotor condition, stator health, air gap integrity, and electrical imbalance without disassembly.
  • Infrared Thermography: Identifies hot spots in switchgear, MCCs, transformers, drives, and terminations before failure occurs.
  • Insulation Resistance & System Testing: Measures insulation condition, identifies contamination or moisture, and verifies electrical integrity.
  • Root-Cause Diagnostics: Post-failure analysis to determine contributing electrical or mechanical factors and prevent recurrence.

​Electrical Testing

Testing can be performed during scheduled outages or while equipment remains in service. All results are documented and delivered in clear, actionable terms. Consider Scheduling a Test If You're Experiencing:​

  • Repeated motor failures
  • Rising vibration or heat
  • Nuisance trips or drive faults
  • Aging equipment with unknown condition

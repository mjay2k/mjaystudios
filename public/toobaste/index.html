Activate · Rachel Holt

The daywalker.

Conservative, but middle-lane — a stealth based icon (a right-leaning Zach Top). 130K on Facebook. And demand's already proven: a fan built an organic group — 180 members, no one running it.

The vehicle

Discord pulls every lever.

Community was the right call — and Discord is the rare platform that pulls all six levers at once: roles & badges, a weekly Live, channels where fans meet each other, direct chat with the artist, and a rally point for the mission.

All six — at once

The architecture

Artist servers,
not one Baste server.

People join a community for the artist, not the label. Rachel's faith-and-family crowd and Dom's young-men brotherhood are different worlds — one room muddies both. So: a server per artist, with a Baste channel inside each for cross-promotion.

Levers

Getting her fans there.

  • Whitespace. Country artists are barely on Discord yet — first-mover, and it's built to sell + host community natively.
  • The objection: her fans are older & Facebook-native. So we don't force a migration.
  • The funnel: Facebook stays the free top (nurture that 180-member group) → Discord is the paid inner circle for the most committed.
  • Frictionless onboarding for older fans — a simple "how to join" so no one's lost.

Dom's community

The Brotherhood.

A place for young based men — faith, self-improvement, accountability, belonging. Young men are the loneliest demographic in America, and no one serves them. Dom leading that is magnetic — and sticky.

Levers

Stream once. Post everywhere.

  • Dom streams raw on Kick — less saturated than Twitch, more viral upside.
  • Every stream feeds a content engine (start with off-the-shelf AI tools, fold into the Hub later) → best moments become YT Shorts; standouts get full videos.
  • Cross-pollinate constantly: produced clips drive back to Kick for the full stream, and promote live events.
  • One stream → a week of content.

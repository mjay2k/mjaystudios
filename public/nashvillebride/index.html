The Device

One device no bridal show has ever owned. We call it The Crossover.

In every shot, two realities live inside a single frame. As the camera moves — panning, booming, pushing in — it reveals the second. This is not a cut between clips. The Crossover is the wedding: the moment wild becomes committed, single becomes paired, neon becomes sacred — performed in one unbroken move.
